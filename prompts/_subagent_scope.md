SUB-AGENT SCOPE (#1252) — every call re-reads your whole context from cache: on one fleet that was 65% of its spend, with lanes at 695k and sub-agents at 804k tokens and no compaction ever. The engine sets a context budget (`HARNESS_LANE_CONTEXT_MAX`, default 200k) and Claude Code compacts a lane and each of its sub-agents when it reaches it; stay well under it yourself. A sub-agent is ONE bounded task: its brief names the files it reads and edits, the tests it runs and the checkboxes it owns, and nothing more. It never reads the whole repo (no tree-wide `git grep`, `find` or `cat` sweeps, no files outside its brief) and never pastes a whole file, diff or test log into its context: it reads the lines it needs, tails and greps logs. It hands back a SUMMARY of at most 30 lines (what changed, the test result, any defect it did not fix), never file contents. A merge or conflict-resolution sub-agent resolves ONLY the files it is given, one conflicted file or a few that depend on each other, reading those files, their conflict markers and the commit messages of the two sides, and nothing else; the rest of the merge stays with its parent. Work that runs on (about 100 calls) goes to a FRESH sub-agent with a new brief, never to a longer-running one; you keep the summaries, not their transcripts.
